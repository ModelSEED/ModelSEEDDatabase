\subsection{Atom mapping and connectivity}\label{sec:results-mapping}

Atom mappings are published for 32,877 reactions, 59\% of the database
(Figure~\ref{fig:direction}C). Of those, 25,058 are clean --- every raw
correspondence was already canonical --- and 7,819 required salvage and are
tagged as such, because a reaction needing one repair is likely to carry others
that are not visible. Reachability and neighbourhood analyses can use both
classes; mechanism-level tracing should filter to the clean set.

Structure-aware similarity resolves reactions expressed in different
namespaces into single equivalence classes, and the resulting ontology is
what allows a model built against one source's identifiers to be compared with
another's. Counts are in Supplementary Table~S3.
