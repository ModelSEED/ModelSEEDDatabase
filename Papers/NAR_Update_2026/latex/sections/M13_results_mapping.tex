\subsection{Atom mapping}\label{sec:results-mapping}

Atom mappings are published for 32,877 reactions, 59\% of the database: 25,058 clean, and 7,819 that required salvage (which are tagged). As the mappings can be used to span a metabolic reconstruction, reactions needing one repair are likely to carry others.
