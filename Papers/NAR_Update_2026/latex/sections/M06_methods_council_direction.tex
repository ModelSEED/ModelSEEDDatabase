\subsection{Grading reaction direction}\label{sec:methods-grading}

We apply a classification approach with which we grade the predicted reaction direction based on the quality of available evidence.
The selected tiers are intuitively described as gold (highest), silver (intermediate), or bronze (lowest).
Our evaluation splits two ways: 1) confidence of a source's own claim fit as a probability against the experimental anchors and 2) a weighted comparison of other sources on the same scale.
Grades, self-assessments, and cross-source verdicts are stored in the biochemistry database and exposed on the website for users inspection.
The grading methodology is thoroughly described in Supplementary Methods~S3.

\subsection{Ensemble LLMs predictions}\label{sec:llm-grading}
Thermodynamic assignment is bounded by estimate coverage, which means that energies and therefore directions cannot be computed for 23,790 structurally incomplete reactions.
We therefore run a complementary approach using an ensemble or "council" of large language models (LLMs) to estimate directionality from only reaction name and stoichiometry, where three LLM agents independently predict a direction, a fourth audits, and a fifth adjudicates.
We provide the prompts in the repository, and describe the process and its limitations in Supplementary Methods~S4.
