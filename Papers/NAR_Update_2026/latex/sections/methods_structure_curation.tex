\subsection{Structure curation}\label{sec:structure-curation}

Structures are the substrate for everything downstream --- balance, atom
mapping and thermodynamics all fail without them --- so a compound with
conflicting structures across sources is worse than one with none.

At the 2020 release a formula disagreement between sources silently dropped the
compound from the unified structure file, which in turn made every reaction
containing it impossible to balance. The updated pipeline classifies each
conflict and routes it to one of three outcomes: an automatic pick from the
source variant matching the historical formula, a curator-authored override
with recorded rationale, or an entry in a mass-balance exclusion registry for
compounds where no source formula is defensible. Every outcome is attributed to
its curator and its evidence.

Structures are stored as InChI, InChIKey and SMILES, canonicalised with RDKit,
at the protonation state appropriate to pH~7. Per-source structures are
retained alongside the unified choice so that a disagreement remains
inspectable rather than being resolved away. Supplementary Methods~S2 gives the
conflict taxonomy and the per-round curation record.
