\subsection{Growth and coverage}\label{sec:results-growth}

The database has grown 34\% in non-obsolete compounds and reactions since 2020, driven mainly by MetaCyc and the BioCyc family.
Reaction growth is now split across three primary databases (Figure~\ref{fig:growth}B): MetaCyc carries 26,140 reactions of which 18,893 are unique among the primary sources, KEGG 10,840 of which 5,291 are unique, and the newly-integrated Rhea 14,511 of which 8,210 are unique.
The completeness of reactions, meaning whether all involved compounds are structurally defined, differs among (Figure~\ref{fig:growth}C): 90\% for MetaCyc, 75\% for KEGG, 65\% for Rhea.
Overall structure coverage rose from 28,087 to 36,907 compounds, with the distribution of this new biochemistry described in Supplementary Methods~S5 and Supplementary Figure~S1.
