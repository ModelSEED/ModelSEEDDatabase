\subsection{Growth and coverage}\label{sec:results-growth}

The database has grown 34\% in compounds and 55\% in reactions since 2020, driven mainly by MetaCyc and the BioCyc family.
Reaction growth is now split across three primary databases (Figure~\ref{fig:growth}B): MetaCyc carries 31,805 reactions of which 21,258 are unique among the primary sources, KEGG carries 14,066 of which 5,756 are unique, and the newly-integrated Rhea carries 17,477 of which 8,339 are unique.
The proportion of "complete" reactions (reactions with all of their constituent molecules structurally defined) completeness differs cross these three sources (Figure~\ref{fig:growth}C): 90\% for MetaCyc, 76\% for KEGG, and 66\% for Rhea.
Overall structure coverage rose from 28,120 to 36,943 compounds.
