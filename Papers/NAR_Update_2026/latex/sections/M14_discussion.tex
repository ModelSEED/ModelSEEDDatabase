\section{Discussion}

Unlike the 2020 release, this update integrates four thermodynamics sources, each with their own uncertainty and estimated direction, and a layered scheme to arrive at a predicted direction where sources disagree in ways a single source conceals.
We further leverage experimental ground truth data to validate our grading of the recommended direction.
In being explicit about disagreement between sources, we expect this release to serve the widening range of researchers who build on shared biochemistry: genome-scale reconstruction, flux analysis, pathway design and the training of predictive models.
