\subsection{Growth in compounds and reactions}

The database has grown substantially since the 2020 release, driven mainly by
contributions from MetaCyc and the BioCyc-family databases (Table~\ref{tab:growth}).
Compound counts grew 34\% (33,992 $\rightarrow$ 45,708), with structure coverage
rising from 28,120 to 36,943 in absolute terms while the coverage rate slipped
one percentage point (83\% $\rightarrow$ 81\%) because new compounds are ingested
faster than the curation pipeline can add structures. Reaction counts grew 55\%
(36,193 $\rightarrow$ 56,012), with balanced-reaction counts growing 35\%
(25,457 $\rightarrow$ 34,370). The balanced-share of reactions regressed from
70\% to 61\%, reflecting the reaction-count growth outpacing balance-restoring
curation; the structure-curation and protein-carrier-cofactor-standardisation
pipelines described in Methods are designed to recover this gap, and the impact
is quantified in the following section.
