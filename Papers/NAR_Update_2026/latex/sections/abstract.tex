The ModelSEED Biochemistry Database (\modelseedurl) supplies foundational mass- and charge-balanced reaction networks for metabolic reconstructions. For this update, the database has expanded to 45,708 compounds and 56,012 reactions, featuring 36,943 curated structures and 34,370 mass-balanced reactions. To ensure data integrity, a new conflict-resolution pipeline reconciles structures across sources, documenting input from curators. Furthermore, we have redesigned the thermodynamic layer. Instead of promoting a single estimate of the direction a reaction may proceed in, we publish predictions from multiple independent sources with their respective uncertainties and estimates of reaction directions based on each one. By grading the available evidence, users can transparently weigh reliability; we classify reactions at three different tiers: ``gold'', ``silver'', and ``bronze''. This multi-source approach exposes discrepancies between sources, including tenfold differences in uncertainty scales. We also provide atom mappings for 32,877 reactions. Our work is publicly available, transparent and modular (\url{https://github.com/ModelSEED/ModelSEEDDatabase}) and moving forward we intend to expand our curation and incorporate more sources of evidence.
