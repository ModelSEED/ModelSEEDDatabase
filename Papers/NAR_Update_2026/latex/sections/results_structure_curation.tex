\subsection{Structure curation}\label{sec:results-structure}

Applied to the priority set of roughly 9,000 reactions used in the v7.0
templates and PlantSEED, the conflict pipeline resolved every previously
conflicting compound in three curation rounds. Fifty-two hydrogen-only
conflicts were matched automatically to the historical formula; the remainder
required curator judgement, and four compounds --- ascorbate radical,
quercetin 3,3'-bissulfate, a Co(I)-corrinoid iron-sulfur protein and a
pyruvate-kinase phosphoserine residue --- were entered in the mass-balance
exclusion registry because no source formula was defensible.

Fifty-six formula-conflict compounds remain across the whole database, none of
them in the priority set, and the balanced-reaction rate on that set held at
8,683 of 9,000 across all three rounds with no regressions.
