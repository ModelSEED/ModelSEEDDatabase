\subsection{Construction and Curation}\label{sec:methods-construction}

We collated additional compounds and reactions from MetaCyc~\cite{caspi2020}, Rhea~\cite{bansal2022}, and ChEBI~\cite{hastings2016}, merging compounds via structures where possible and matching reactions by compound identities, similar to the 2020 release~\cite{seaver2020}.
Mass and charge balances are enforced and flagged.
Unlike the 2020 release, where compounds with conflicting sourced structures were either manually reviewed or silently dropped, the updated pipeline classifies structural conflicts and either 1) automatically picks from the source that matches the historical formula, 2) prioritizes curator authored and justified content, or 3) exclusion (with logged justification) for compounds without a defensible source formula.
This enforces that every outcome is attributed to its curator and evidence.
Every structure is retained in the repository to facilitate conflict resolution.
Investigators can contribute to the ModelSEED Database through the procedure in Supplementary Methods~S1, which is expanded in the repository.
