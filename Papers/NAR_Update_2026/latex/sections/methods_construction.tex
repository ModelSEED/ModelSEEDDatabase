\subsection{Database construction}\label{sec:methods-construction}

Compounds and reactions are collated from KEGG, MetaCyc and the BioCyc family,
ChEBI, Rhea, BiGG and MetaNetX, reconciled on structure where one is available
and on identifier otherwise, and published with per-source attribution on every
record. Transport reactions, compounds of undetermined structure, and the
release and versioning procedure follow the 2020 release~\cite{seaver2020} and
are described in Supplementary Methods~S1.

Mass and charge balance is enforced at build time rather than audited
afterwards. A reaction that cannot be balanced from its compounds' formulae is
flagged and excluded from the balanced set rather than silently adjusted; the
count of such reactions is reported below and is the principal quality metric
for the collation.
