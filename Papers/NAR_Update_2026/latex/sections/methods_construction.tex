\subsection{Database construction}\label{sec:methods-construction}

Compounds and reactions are collated from KEGG, MetaCyc and the BioCyc family,
ChEBI, Rhea, BiGG and MetaNetX, reconciled on structure where one exists and on
identifier otherwise, and published with per-source attribution on every
record. Transport reactions, compounds of undetermined structure, and the
release procedure follow the 2020 release~\cite{seaver2020}.

Mass and charge balance is enforced at build time rather than audited
afterwards: a reaction that cannot be balanced from its compounds' formulae is
flagged and excluded from the balanced set rather than silently adjusted.
