\section{Where the growth since 2020 landed}\label{sec:s5}

Central metabolism has been characterised for decades, so the question is where a 2026 reconstruction still gains information. Supplementary Figure~\ref{fig:s1} places the 12,261 reactions added since the 2020 release by MetaCyc pathway class against the 36,142 carried over, counting non-obsolete reactions on both sides. The join is the same for both eras: ModelSEED identifier $\rightarrow$ MetaCyc reaction, through the released alias file, $\rightarrow$ pathway $\rightarrow$ parent class, one level up, through the MetaCyc pathway table shipped with the database; the organisational class \emph{Super-Pathways} is excluded. Reading the pre-2020 side from the released pathway annotation instead would compare a full ancestor closure against a one-level join, so it is not done. The join places 2,282 of the additions and 12,518 of the carried-over reactions; the 8,409 reactions that arrived through Rhea have no pathway ontology to be placed in and are absent rather than counted as unannotated.

The classes that grew are those of specialised metabolism. O-antigen biosynthesis gained 218 reactions against 29 already held, branched-chain fatty-acid biosynthesis 98 against 8 and lipid IV-A biosynthesis 37 against 6, while antibiotic biosynthesis (265 against 740), polyketides (169 against 214), toxins (142 against 192) and lipids (140 against 281) are enriched two- to four-fold among the additions relative to their share of what was already held. The individual pathways that gained most are the mycolate biosynthesis superpathway (57 reactions), arachidonate metabolite biosynthesis (37), colibactin biosynthesis (34) and the three branched-chain fatty-acid pathways (32 each). Central carbon and energy metabolism --- energy metabolism, chemoautotrophic energy metabolism, electron transfer, fermentation, the TCA variants, glycolysis, the pentose phosphate cycle, photosynthesis, respiration and methanogenesis --- account for 41 of the 12,261 additions, against 328 of the 36,142 reactions already held. Central metabolism was already saturated in 2020; what a 2026 reconstruction gains is the periphery. The counts are produced by \texttt{analysis/pathway\_distribution\_of\_growth.py} under \texttt{Papers/NAR\_Update\_2026}, which reads only the released files and the repository's history.
