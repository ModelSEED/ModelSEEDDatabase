## Narrative for the Results section

- The database has grown substantially since 2020 (+34% compounds, +55% reactions), driven mainly by MetaCyc contributions (+6,600 compounds, +10,000 reactions). KEGG barely moved.
- **Balanced-reaction share regressed from 70% to 61%.** This is the load-bearing hook the paper's structure-curation and protein-carrier-cofactor standardization sections should recover. The recovery, measured before/after the §5 pass, is Results-section headline material.
- Structure-coverage rate slipped one percentage point (83% → 81%). New compounds are being ingested faster than the PubChem-pipeline + curator-override system can add structures, but the absolute number of compounds with structures grew by 8,823.
