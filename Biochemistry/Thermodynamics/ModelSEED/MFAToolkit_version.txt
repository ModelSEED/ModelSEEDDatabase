      * H2/H3 phosphates-shadow bugs in _walk_stoichiometry (031122af
        upstream in Cooper Taylor's branch, ported to
        reversibility_heuristics.py in this repo).
      * Species::FindCycles per-molecule timeout lowered from 300s to
        30s (staging SHA 7045882). Batch runs over 25K compounds
        previously spent 72 h at 300s each on the ~865 pathological

        30s and 30-min budgets, neither of which completed cycle
        enumeration — the "value" was pure artifact of how far the
        DFS walker had gotten.
        16 total cycle-finder timeouts across the four runs =
        0.02% of the ~83K attempted compounds; those now land as
        sentinel rows treated correctly downstream as "no GC value".
